Privacy Policy

N8n Rag Agent · Last updated: October 28, 2026

Before publishing: Review this policy against your actual n8n workflow and Google OAuth scopes. Update any statement that does not accurately describe your setup, and add a real contact email.

1. About this project

N8n Rag Agent is an educational prototype intended to answer questions using a supplied faculty-responsibility knowledge-base document. It is an independent project and is not an official college service.

2. Information that may be processed

Depending on how the workflow is configured, the service may process questions entered by users, text retrieved from the knowledge-base document, and information made available through a Google account connection. The exact information accessible depends on the permissions granted and the nodes enabled in the n8n workflow.

3. How information is used

Information is used to retrieve relevant passages from the configured knowledge base and generate responses. Google OAuth is used to authorize the Google services and permissions requested by the workflow. This page does not imply that the app accesses all Google Drive files; access depends on the granted scopes and workflow configuration.

4. Storage, logs, and third-party services

Information may be handled by Google, n8n, and any other services or model providers connected in the workflow. Their handling of information is subject to their own terms and privacy policies. Depending on the workflow settings, prompts, outputs, document content, execution logs, or credentials metadata may be retained by connected services. The workflow operator should check execution history, logging, and retention settings and configure them appropriately.

5. Sharing and sale

This template makes no blanket guarantee about data sharing or retention. Before using the app with real or confidential information, the operator must verify every connected service and document the actual data flows here. Do not submit confidential, sensitive, or personal information unless the operator has confirmed it is appropriate to do so.

6. Security and permissions

Grant only the Google permissions needed for the workflow. Access can be revoked through your Google Account's third-party connections settings. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

This prototype is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been submitted unnecessarily, contact the project operator.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the workflow or its connected services change. The date above should be revised when updates are made.
